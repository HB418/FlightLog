Flight/Course Ratings

This is an original rating system, not PDGA's. PDGA's real ratings need a live field of rated players competing on the same day to work at all. Since a small app doesn't have that player pool, this system rates a course by its own physical layout instead, so a single player can get a real rating the very first time they play a course, alone.

Course Rating

Every course has a running difficulty multiplier. It starts at 1.00. In the Admin panel, each hole can be flagged with any of four hazards: Dogleg, Water, Trees, Out of Bounds. Every checked hazard, on every hole, adds +0.05 to the course's multiplier — cumulative across the whole course, never averaged or reset.

Course Rating = (Total Length × Multiplier) ÷ Total Par

Baseline Score

This is the score a hypothetical "1000-rated" player would be expected to shoot on this specific course. It starts from what the course's rating would be with zero hazards (length ÷ par alone), then converts the extra difficulty your hazards add into extra strokes (every 15 points of hazard difficulty = 1 extra stroke), added on top of par.

Round Rating

Every round you finish is rated against that course's Baseline Score, at 10 rating points per stroke:

Round Rating = 1000 − ((Your Score − Baseline Score) × 10)

Shoot exactly the baseline score → 1000. Beat it → above 1000. Come in over it → below 1000.

Your Flight Rating

Shown on your Account page and in Stats. It's a simple average of every Round Rating you've earned across every round you've played. It starts at 1000 before you've played anything.

Since this system doesn't compare against other real players the way PDGA/UDisc do, don't expect your number here to match a rating from those apps — it's measuring you against this app's own course-difficulty math, consistently, every time.
